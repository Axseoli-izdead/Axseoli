My Confession to myself


To speak of what I have been denying to myself all my life. "I am afraid of people not just in ordinary but explicitly am terrified of them that my body trembles in sight of them. So terrified as I was that I always find myself looking down and guess who it was, as if I can't picture them as a whole and unknowingly it became a habit of mine."Although I wasn't entirely like this in the past or so I thought—I have been equivocating and coercing myself that I'll grow out of it but I never did. In converse of it, it only fueled my anxiety and leading my life more miserable. I am ashamed of myself on how I made my life more miserable, how existentially dreadful I am to myself. I found myself guilty in denying how obtuse I have become. I desperately hid the fact that I am a social outcast, an alien to society. Here I stand smiling cheerfully, I dare say that I am afraid of human beings even to myself .


References: -Osamu Dazai & Fyodor Dostoevsky

About the Author

To speak of what I have been denying to myself all my life, I have spent a long time looking down and guessing who I was, feeling like an alien to society and masking my profound anxieties beneath a smiling face. I am Amber C. Rovilla, 18 years old, and this is a literature that i wrote to express myself and connecting myself to Osamu Dazai & Fyodor Dostoevsky-writers who understand what it means to be a social outcast and to live being a utterly terrified of human beings while desperately seeking to comprehend them. This is an archive of my own autofiction, a place where I confess about myself. Writing this is a method of turning my deepest vulnerabilities and existential fears into something inspirational that even in those vulnerabilities can create something poetic, that can capture the heavy thoughts that are too difficult to speak aloud.
