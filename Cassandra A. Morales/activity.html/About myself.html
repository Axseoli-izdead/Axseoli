About myself

Get to Know Me!

I started my journey as a competitive swimmer when I was around 8 or 9 years old. It has been one of the most memorable experiences of my life because swimming is not just about competing and winning medals; it’s also about meeting new friends, creating wonderful memories, and learning valuable lessons. Through the years, it taught me discipline, determination, confidence, and sportsmanship. Although I had to step away from competitive swimming when I entered college, the experiences and friendships I gained will always hold a special place in my heart.

Another passion of mine is photography, which has been part of my life for seven years as a photojournalist. I enjoy capturing meaningful moments, telling stories through pictures, and seeing the world from different perspectives. Above all, photojournalism is not just about telling stories; it is also about uncovering the truth, presenting facts, and giving people a glimpse of realities that deserve to be seen and heard. Being involved in photojournalism has allowed me to express my creativity, improve my skills, and understand the importance of truth and responsibility in every photograph. Both swimming and photography have given me unforgettable memories and helped shape the person I am today.
